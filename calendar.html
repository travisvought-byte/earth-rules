What comes due, month by month.

The deadlines nobody reminds you about, in the order they arrive.

Month by month

Dates are for the federal government. When a date lands on a weekend or holiday it moves to the next business day.

January

  • Employers must send W-2s, and most 1099s are due to you, by January 31.
  • Fourth-quarter estimated tax is due January 15 if you are self-employed.
  • HealthCare.gov open enrollment ends January 15; enrolling after December 15 starts coverage February 1.

February

  • Gather tax papers and choose how to file. Free options are on the taxes page.
  • Check your credit reports; you can get them free every week.

March

  • If you expect a refund, filing early gets it to you sooner.
  • Spring clock change: test smoke and carbon monoxide alarms.

April

  • Federal income tax returns are due April 15, and most states, including Ohio, use the same day.
  • First-quarter estimated tax is due April 15.
  • Last day to put money in an IRA for the year before.
  • Need more time? An extension gives you until October 15 to file, but not to pay.

May

  • Renew anything that expires in summer before travel season: passport, license, registration.

June

  • Second-quarter estimated tax is due June 15.

July

  • Mid-year check: is enough tax being withheld? The IRS withholding estimator will tell you.

August

  • Back to school: immunization records and school forms.
  • Many employers start benefits open enrollment in the fall; watch for the notice.

September

  • Third-quarter estimated tax is due September 15.

October

  • October 15 is the last day to file if you got an extension.
  • Medicare open enrollment runs October 15 to December 7.
  • Flu season starts; ask a pharmacist or doctor about a flu shot.

November

  • HealthCare.gov open enrollment starts November 1.
  • Fall clock change: test smoke and carbon monoxide alarms.
  • Election day is the first Tuesday after the first Monday. Check your registration a month ahead.

December

  • Pick a HealthCare.gov plan by December 15 for coverage that starts January 1.
  • Medicare open enrollment ends December 7.
  • Use or lose: check flexible spending account deadlines and any year-end benefits.

Once a year, any time

  • A checkup and dental cleaning. See which screenings fit your age on the checkups page.
  • Look at all three credit reports. Weekly free reports are on the credit page.
  • Check who is named as beneficiary on retirement accounts and life insurance.
  • Renew your vehicle registration. In many states it follows your birthday; see your state.
  • Review your insurance: car, renters or home, and health.
