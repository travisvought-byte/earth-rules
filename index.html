Why this exists

Nobody hands you a manual. School covers the quadratic formula and skips how to file a tax return, build credit, change your address or make a friend after college. Earth Rules puts those things in one place, in plain words, with a link to the official source for every rule and the date it was checked.

Where someone already explains a topic well, such as the IRS, USA.gov or Khan Academy, we link to them instead of rewriting it.
