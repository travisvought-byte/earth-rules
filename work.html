Find work.

Free programs that lead to real jobs, and how to spot fake ones.

Money and papersTopic 3 of 12

Programs that are real

These are run or funded by the government, cost you nothing, and lead to actual jobs. If a "job" asks you to pay first, cash a check for them or buy gift cards, it's a scam.

Everywhere

  • Skilled trades, health care and driving jobs are in demand almost everywhere. Training is often short and sometimes paid.
  • Every country has a public job service. Search for its name plus "employment service."

In the United States

  • American Job Centers: free career help, and training paid for by the WIOA program if you qualify. Find one at CareerOneStop (opens in a new tab).
  • Apprenticeships: get paid while you learn a trade. Apprenticeship.gov (opens in a new tab).
  • Job Corps: free training, and often housing, for ages 16 to 24. JobCorps.gov (opens in a new tab).
  • Veterans: VA Veteran Readiness and Employment (opens in a new tab) for service-connected disabilities, and priority service at every job center.

In Ohio

  • OhioMeansJobs: job listings, resume help and training money at OhioMeansJobs.com (opens in a new tab) and centers in every county.
  • Disability: Opportunities for Ohioans with Disabilities (opens in a new tab) pays for training and job help. Apply online or call 800-282-4536.
