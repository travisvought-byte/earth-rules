EARTH RULES · OHIO

Ohio.

The Ohio rules most people run into, with the official link for each.

Driving and your car

  • Moved within Ohio? Tell the BMV within 10 days (Ohio Revised Code 4507.09). You can do it online at the Ohio BMV (opens in a new tab).
  • New to Ohio? Get an Ohio driver's license within 30 days of becoming a resident.
  • License renewal: choose a 4-year or 8-year license. People 65 and older get 4 years.

Taxes

Ohio has a state income tax, due the same day as the federal return. Many cities and school districts add their own. Free filing: Ohio Department of Taxation (opens in a new tab).

Voting

Register at least 30 days before an election, at the Ohio Secretary of State (opens in a new tab). You can register at 17 if you will be 18 by the general election.

Your rights in Ohio

Every page in Your rights lists what Ohio adds to federal law: as a renter, worker, patient, voter, veteran and more.

Help

  • Ohio Benefits (opens in a new tab): food, health coverage and cash assistance applications.
  • Ohio Legal Help (opens in a new tab): free legal information and help finding a lawyer.
  • Dial 211 for local help anywhere in Ohio.
