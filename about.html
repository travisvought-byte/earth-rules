About Earth Rules.

Who runs it, where the facts come from, and how to report a mistake.

What this is

Earth Rules is a free guide to the things everyone is expected to know and do as an adult: the yearly deadlines, the one-time steps, the people skills and the averages. It starts with the United States and Ohio and will add every state, then other countries.

Who runs it

Earth Rules is created and maintained by Travis Vought. There is no paid placement. Organizations we link to are not partners unless this page says so.

Where the information comes from

Every page lists its sources, mostly official government pages, and the date they were checked. Health pages give general information from public health agencies and point you to a clinician. If something is wrong, email travis@vethomeguard.org.

Accessibility

The site is built to WCAG 2.1 AA: it works with a keyboard alone, has a skip link, labels every icon with text beside it, and keeps color contrast high. If anything is hard to use, email us and we will fix it.
