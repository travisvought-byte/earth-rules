Turning 18: the checklist.

The handful of things that become yours to handle.

Your timelineTopic 1 of 7

The checklist

  • Register to vote. In many states, including Ohio, you can register before 18 if you will be 18 by the next general election. Vote.gov (opens in a new tab) sends you to your state's form.
  • Selective Service: almost all men ages 18 through 25, citizens and immigrants, must be registered, within 30 days of turning 18. A 2025 law moves the government to automatic registration; until your registration shows on SSS.gov (opens in a new tab), register yourself. It takes a few minutes.
  • Get a state ID or driver's license if you don't have one. Keep your birth certificate and Social Security card in a safe place, not your wallet.
  • Open a bank account in your own name, and start credit carefully. See the credit page.
  • Health insurance: you can usually stay on a parent's plan until 26.
  • First job: you'll fill out a W-4 for tax withholding and an I-9 to show you can work. Keep your pay stubs.
  • Learn the basics free: Khan Academy financial literacy (opens in a new tab) and GCFGlobal (opens in a new tab).

Jury duty

Once you're on the voter or license rolls, you can be summoned for jury duty. A summons is a court order: answer it, and ask the court about a postponement if the date doesn't work.
