EARTH RULES · LIFE STAGES

Life stages.

What changes at each stage, and what becomes yours to handle.

Pick a stage

Turning 18

Vote, Selective Service, ID, first bank account, first job papers.

Your twenties

Taxes, credit, renting, your own health insurance by 26, retirement savings at your first job.

Starting a family

Birth certificate and Social Security number for the baby, insurance changes within the special enrollment window, a will.

Buying a home

The median first-time buyer is 40. Credit, down payment and what normal looks like.

Mid-life

Screenings start: colorectal at 45, mammograms from 40, diabetes from 35 if overweight.

Retirement

Medicare at 65, Social Security timing, and USA.gov's retirement benefit finder.
