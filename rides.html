EARTH RULES · RIDES

Getting around.

Cheap and free rides to work, the doctor and the store.

Everywhere

  • Buses and trains are the cheapest way to get around in most places. Ask about reduced fares for older people, people with disabilities and students.
  • Carpools and vanpools split the cost of a commute. Many cities and employers will match you with riders.

In the United States

  • Reduced fares: bus systems that take federal money must offer half fare at off-peak times to older adults and people with disabilities.
  • Paratransit: if a disability keeps you from using the bus, your transit system must offer door-to-door rides in the same area. Ask your transit agency to apply.
  • Older adults: the Eldercare Locator (opens in a new tab), 800-677-1116, finds senior ride programs near you.
  • Veterans: many VA medical centers run free shuttles and van programs to appointments. Call your VA clinic.

In Ohio

  • Medicaid rides: call the member number on your card at least two days ahead. Your county Job and Family Services office can also help.
  • Dial 211 for local ride programs.
