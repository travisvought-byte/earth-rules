EARTH RULES · MOVING

Moving: the checklist.

The address changes people forget, and the deadlines that come with them.

HomeTopic 5 of 6

Before you move

  • Forward your mail with the Postal Service change of address (opens in a new tab).
  • Renters insurance for the new place; landlords often require it.
  • Read the lease before you sign: rent, deposit, who fixes what, how to end it.
  • Photograph the old and new place on move-out and move-in day to protect your deposit.

After you move

  • Update your driver's license and vehicle registration. Deadlines are short: Ohio gives you 10 days for an address change and new residents 30 days to get an Ohio license. See your state.
  • Update your voter registration at Vote.gov (opens in a new tab).
  • Tell the IRS with Form 8822 if you won't file a return soon, and update banks, employer, insurance and any benefits.
  • Moving can open a special enrollment period for health coverage at HealthCare.gov (opens in a new tab).
