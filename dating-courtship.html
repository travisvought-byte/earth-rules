Dating and courtship.

Dating is how many people get to know a possible partner, and every culture does it a little differently.

Love and familyTopic 1 of 9

In 10 seconds

What it is: Dating is spending time with someone to see if you want a closer relationship. Courtship is the more serious stage that can lead to marriage.

Why it matters: Knowing how it usually works, and how to stay safe, helps you enjoy it and spot trouble early.

Do this first

  1. Meet people through friends, clubs, classes, faith groups, volunteering or apps.
  2. Keep your address, workplace and money details private until you really trust someone.
  3. Meet in a busy public place and tell a friend where you are going.
  4. Never send money to someone you have not met in person.
  5. Ask before you touch, and respect a no. You can leave a date any time.

Meeting people

Most people meet partners through friends, school, work, hobbies, faith groups and volunteering. Dating apps and sites are common too. Doing things you enjoy is the easiest way to meet people who like the same things. See making friends for ideas.

Early dating

  • Early dates are usually short and low cost: coffee, a walk, a meal, an event.
  • Talk, listen, and be yourself. You are both just finding out if you like each other.
  • Go at a pace that feels right to you. You never owe anyone a second date, a kiss or anything else.
  • Get your own ride, so you can leave when you want.
  • Consent means a clear, free yes, every time, and you can change your mind. Read sex ed and consent.
  • Know the warning signs of control or abuse. Read dating red flags.

App safety and romance scams

Scammers make fake profiles on dating apps and social media. The Federal Trade Commission (FTC) says to watch for these signs:

  • They quickly want to move the chat off the app.
  • They always have a reason they cannot meet, like working far away or being in the military.
  • They ask for money for travel, medical bills or an emergency, or offer to help you invest in crypto.

Do a reverse image search of their profile photo. Search their job plus the word "scammer". If friends or family are worried, listen to them. Report scams to the app and to the FTC at ReportFraud.ftc.gov (opens in a new tab). See scams for more.

Courtship around the world

How people find a partner differs a lot from place to place, and even within one family or faith. Some common ways:

  • Personal choice. In many places, young adults date and choose their own partner, and people expect love to come before marriage.
  • Family introductions and arranged matches. In many families, parents or elders help choose or introduce a partner. Often a go-between, called a matchmaker, looks for a match that suits both families.
  • A mix of both. In many places, couples and families now decide together. In India, for example, most marriages have been arranged by family elders, while marriages for love are becoming more common in cities.
  • Apps and websites. Used for both casual dating and serious, marriage-minded matches.

Whatever the custom, both people must freely agree. A marriage someone is pushed into is forced marriage, which is different. See weddings and customs.

Everywhere

Anyone can pretend to be someone else online. Wherever you live, keep personal details private until you trust someone, never send money to a person you met online, meet in public, and know that you can cut a date short and stop at any time without saying sorry.
