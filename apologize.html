Apologize and forgive.

A real apology can repair a lot, and forgiveness can set you free without letting anyone off the hook.

PeopleTopic 3 of 6

In 10 seconds

What it is: How to give an apology that works, how to receive one, and what forgiveness does and doesn't mean.

Why it matters: Everyone messes up, and knowing how to make it right keeps friendships, families and jobs together.

Do this first

  1. Name exactly what you did and who it hurt.
  2. Say "I'm sorry" with no excuses and no "but".
  3. Ask what would help make it right, then do it.
  4. Change the behavior so it doesn't happen again.
  5. Give the other person time. They don't owe you instant forgiveness.

How to apologize

  1. Own it. Say "I made a mistake," not "Mistakes were made." Be specific about what you did.
  2. Keep the explanation short. Long stories sound like excuses. "There's no excuse for what I did" is stronger.
  3. Show you understand the hurt. Say how it affected them.
  4. Make it right. Fix or replace what you can. Ask what would mean the most to them.
  5. Change. Say what you'll do differently, and then do it.

Apologies that don't work

  • "I'm sorry you feel hurt." This blames their feelings, not your actions.
  • "I'm sorry, but you were getting on my nerves." Anything after "but" cancels the apology.
  • Asking to be forgiven right away. Fixing things and owning it matter more than the request.

Receiving an apology

You can say "Thank you for saying that." You don't have to say "It's fine" if it wasn't. You can take time before you decide how you feel. You can also say what you need to rebuild trust.

What forgiveness is

Forgiveness is choosing to let go of resentment so it stops running your life. It is not:

  • Forgetting what happened
  • Saying it was okay
  • Trusting the person again
  • Getting back together or staying in contact

If someone keeps hurting you, staying away may be the healthy choice. You can still forgive in your own heart. Many people find letting go of grudges lowers stress and helps their health.

Everywhere

A sincere apology and a real effort to repair harm help people get along in every culture. See how to disagree and friends for more on getting along.
