EARTH RULES · FINE PRINT

The fine print.

What to look for before you sign, and how to back out.

You can always ask for time

You have the right to read anything before you sign it, take it home, and ask someone else to read it. If they won't let you, that tells you something.

What to look for

  • Auto-renewal

    Free trials that turn into monthly charges, and subscriptions that renew every year.

    Your move: Put the cancel date in your calendar the day you sign up.

  • Arbitration clauses

    You agree not to sue in court or join a class action. Disputes go to a private arbitrator instead, often chosen by the company.

    Your move: Look for an opt-out window, often 30 days, and use it.

  • Fees and penalties

    Late fees, early-cancel fees, "processing" fees and fees for paying by phone.

    Your move: Add up the total cost, not the monthly price.

  • Leases

    Who fixes what, when rent can go up, notice to move out, and what comes out of the deposit.

    Your move: Take dated photos when you move in. See your rights as a renter.

  • Co-signing

    If you co-sign, you owe the whole debt if they don't pay.

    Your move: Only co-sign what you could afford to pay yourself.

  • Privacy

    What they collect about you and who they sell it to.

    Your move: Pick the most private settings.

Changed your mind?

  • US: the FTC's Cooling-Off Rule gives you 3 days to cancel many sales made at your home, or at a temporary location like a hotel or fair.
  • Ohio: Ohio law also gives you until midnight of the third business day to cancel a sale made at your home.
  • Cancel in writing, keep a copy, and note the date.
