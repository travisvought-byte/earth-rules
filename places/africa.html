If you're thinking about suicide or can't cope

Find A Helpline (opens in a new tab) lists free, checked help lines in more than 150 countries. It's run by ThroughLine and backed by the International Association for Suicide Prevention.

Rights everyone has

In 1948 the United Nations agreed on the Universal Declaration of Human Rights (opens in a new tab). It says every person, in every country, is born free and equal in dignity and rights. Countries don't all live up to it, but it's the common starting point.

If you're arrested away from home

You can ask the police to tell your country's consulate. They have to tell you that you can ask. This comes from a treaty almost every country has signed, the Vienna Convention on Consular Relations.

Rights in Africa

54 of the African Union's 55 countries have joined the African Charter on Human and Peoples' Rights. The African Commission in Banjul, The Gambia, hears complaints. The African Court in Arusha, Tanzania, can rule on them, but only a handful of countries let a person bring a case there directly.
