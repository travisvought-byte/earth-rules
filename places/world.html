EARTH RULES · THE WORLD

Everywhere.

What holds anywhere on Earth, and what changes when you cross a border.

Emergency numbers

The number to call for police, fire or ambulance
WhereNumber
United States, Canada and most of the Americas911
European Union (works in every member country)112
United Kingdom999 or 112
Australia000
India112

Most mobile phones will connect 112 or 911 to local emergency services in many countries, but don't count on it. Look up the number when you arrive.

What changes from country to country

  • Health care: most rich countries cover everyone through a public system. Find out how to see a doctor before you need one.
  • Your rights: the local law applies to you, even as a visitor. If you're arrested abroad, you can ask that your country's embassy be told.
  • Driving: which side of the road, whether your license works, and the drinking limit.
  • Money: tipping, taxes in the price or added at the register, and how people pay.
  • Manners: greetings, personal space, time and how direct people are. See how people work.
