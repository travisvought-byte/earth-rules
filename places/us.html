Numbers that work anywhere in the US

  • 911: police, fire, ambulance.
  • 988: Suicide and Crisis Lifeline, call or text. Veterans press 1.
  • 211: food, rent, bills, rides and local programs.
  • 1-800-662-4357: drug and alcohol treatment referrals.
