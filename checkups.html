Checkups and screenings by age.

General guidance from the U.S. Preventive Services Task Force. Your own clinician has the final word.

Screenings by age

Common screenings recommended by the U.S. Preventive Services Task Force
ScreeningWho
Blood pressureAdults 18 and older
DepressionAdults
AnxietyAdults under 65
Hepatitis COnce, ages 18 to 79
HIVAges 15 to 65
Cervical cancerAges 21 to 65, on a schedule set with your clinician
Prediabetes and type 2 diabetesAges 35 to 70 who are overweight
Breast cancer (mammogram)Every 2 years, ages 40 to 74
Colorectal cancerAges 45 to 75
Lung cancerAges 50 to 80 with a heavy smoking history who smoke now or quit within 15 years

These are general recommendations for people without symptoms. Your clinician may advise something different based on your history.

Every year

  • A checkup with a primary care clinician, and a dental cleaning.
  • Ask which vaccines you're due for; CDC posts the adult schedule and pharmacies can give most of them.

Reading about a condition

MedlinePlus (opens in a new tab), from the National Library of Medicine, explains conditions, tests and medicines in plain language. It's a place to learn, not a diagnosis. Bring your questions to a doctor, nurse or pharmacist.
