What this site collects, which is very little.

Privacy

  • There is no sign-in, and nothing you choose on this site is saved or sent anywhere.
  • A visit counter, GoatCounter, counts page views without cookies. It records the page, the site you came from, your browser and screen size, and an approximate country. It does not store your IP address.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep server logs, including IP addresses, for security.
  • Links to other sites go to organizations with their own privacy practices.
