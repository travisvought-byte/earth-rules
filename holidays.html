World holidays.

People around the world mark the year with holidays. Some fall on the same date every year, and some move.

World and cultureTopic 6 of 8

In 10 seconds

What it is: A calendar of major religious, cultural and national holidays, month by month.

Why it matters: Knowing when holidays fall helps you plan work, school and visits, and wish people well at the right time.

Do this first

  1. Add your coworkers' and classmates' holidays to your calendar.
  2. Check the exact date each year for holidays that move.
  3. Avoid setting big meetings or tests on major holy days.
  4. Ask people how they celebrate and what greeting they like.
  5. Plan ahead for closed banks and offices on federal holidays.

Why some dates move

Most of the world uses a calendar based on the sun. Many faiths also use calendars based on the moon.

  • Islamic holidays follow a moon calendar of about 354 days. So Ramadan and the two Eids come about 11 days earlier each year and pass through every season.
  • Jewish, Hindu, Buddhist and Chinese holidays also follow the moon, but they stay in about the same season each year.
  • Easter is the first Sunday after the first full moon after the spring equinox. It falls between March 22 and April 25. Orthodox Easter often comes later.

Some communities start these months when the new moon is sighted, so check with your local mosque, temple or synagogue for the exact date.

A few customs

  • Diwali lasts five days. Many families light clay oil lamps, decorate floors with rangoli designs, visit relatives, share gifts and food, and set off fireworks.
  • Holi starts with a bonfire the night before. The next day, people throw colored powder and water, then clean up and visit friends and family.
  • Vesak brings temple visits, offerings of flowers and incense, gifts of food to monks, and lanterns in some countries.
  • Passover families clear their homes of leavened bread before the holiday and retell the Exodus story at the seder.
  • Christmas customs include decorated trees, carols, cards and gifts. In much of Europe gifts are opened on December 24, and in North America on December 25.

Everywhere

Holidays are times for family, food, faith and rest almost everywhere. If you are not sure whether someone celebrates a holiday, a simple "Are you doing anything for the holiday?" is a friendly way to ask. See world religions for the beliefs behind these days, and the year at a glance for planning.

In the United States

Federal law sets 11 public holidays for federal workers. When one falls on a Saturday, most federal workers get the Friday before off. When it falls on a Sunday, they get the Monday after. That is why Independence Day is observed on July 5 and Christmas on December 24 in 2027. If you need time off for a religious holiday, your employer may have to work with you. See world religions.
