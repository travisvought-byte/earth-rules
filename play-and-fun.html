Play and fun.

Play is not a waste of time. It helps kids grow and helps adults stay well.

Skills and funTopic 4 of 4

In 10 seconds

What it is: Play is anything you do for fun, like games, sports, art, music, being outside, or a hobby you love.

Why it matters: Play lowers stress, builds skills and friendships, and a lot of the best fun is free.

Do this first

  1. Get a free library card and look at the program calendar.
  2. Visit a state or national park. Ohio State Parks are free to enter.
  3. Pick one hobby and try it for a month.
  4. Join a group, class or club about something you like.
  5. Set screen time limits and plan screen free time together.

Why play matters

For kids: The American Academy of Pediatrics says play helps children plan, get along with others, handle their feelings, and learn language and math. It also helps them cope with stress. Simple toys like blocks, boxes and puzzles work great. Your attention matters more than costly gadgets. Mix it up: pretend play, running around, outdoor play, and reading and singing together. Some boredom is fine. It sparks creativity.

For adults: The CDC says strong social ties are linked to less stress, better sleep, and a longer, healthier life. Hobbies and groups are an easy way to build them. See friends.

Free and cheap fun

  • Library. A card is usually free if you live in the area. You may need ID or proof of address. Libraries lend books, movies and games, offer computers and internet, and run free programs like story time and summer reading.
  • Parks. Walk, hike, fish, picnic, swim at public beaches, or join a ranger program.
  • Community and senior centers. Many offer classes, open gym, crafts and game nights.
  • Volunteering. Helping out is a great way to meet people and feel good.
  • At home. Card games, board games, cooking, music, drawing, gardening and puzzles.

Screens

Real time with people is better for learning than screens. Try a family media plan with simple rules, like no screens at the dinner table or in bedrooms. See attention for how apps are built to keep you scrolling.

Sports and games for every body

  • Special Olympics offers year round sports training and competition for children and adults with intellectual disabilities. Unified Sports teams mix athletes with and without disabilities. Several state programs say athletes never pay. Find your local program at specialolympics.org.
  • Veterans with disabilities can join VA national adaptive sports events, like the Wheelchair Games, the Winter Sports Clinic, the Summer Sports Clinic and the Golden Age Games. VA also pays a monthly allowance to some veterans with disabilities who train in Paralympic sports. See veterans' rights.

Picking up a hobby

  1. Think of what you liked as a kid, or what you always wanted to try.
  2. Start small and cheap. Borrow gear or books from the library or a friend.
  3. Set a little time each week, like 30 minutes on Saturday.
  4. Find a group: a club, class, league or meetup. Doing it with others makes it stick.
  5. It is fine to quit and try something else. The point is fun.

Everywhere

Children everywhere learn through play, and adults of every age do better with people and activities they enjoy. Being outside, moving your body, making things and sharing time with others are free in almost every place on Earth.

In the United States

National parks. In 2026 the National Park Service had 8 entrance fee free days for US residents, including Memorial Day, the Independence Day weekend and Veterans Day. Check the NPS passes page for next year's dates. Camping and other fees still apply.

Free park passes: the Access Pass for US citizens and residents with a permanent disability, the Military Lifetime Pass for veterans and Gold Star families, the Military Annual Pass for current service members and their dependents, a pass for every 4th grader, and a Volunteer Pass after 250 service hours.

In Ohio

Ohio's 76 state parks have no entrance fee. You can hike, paddle and watch wildlife as much as you want. Camping and some other activities cost money.

The National Veterans Golden Age Games will be held in Cleveland, June 23 to 29, 2027.
