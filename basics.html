Food

  • SNAP puts money for groceries on a card each month. Apply in Ohio at Ohio Benefits (opens in a new tab).
  • WIC gives food, formula and support to pregnant people and children under 5. About WIC (opens in a new tab).
  • Food banks and pantries give food free, no long forms. Dial 211 or search Feeding America (opens in a new tab).

Heat and lights

  • Every state runs a home energy program with federal money (LIHEAP).
  • Ohio: HEAP gives a credit on your heating bill. Apply at energyhelp.ohio.gov (opens in a new tab) or call 800-282-0880.
  • Before a shutoff, call your utility and ask for a payment plan. Most have one.

Phone and internet

  • Lifeline takes up to $9.25 a month off one phone or internet bill if you get SNAP, Medicaid, SSI, housing help or have low income. Apply at LifelineSupport.org (opens in a new tab).
  • Many internet companies sell low-cost plans for families on benefits. Ask for their "low-income plan" by name.

Child care

  • Find licensed child care and help paying at ChildCare.gov (opens in a new tab).
  • Ohio: your county Job and Family Services office handles publicly funded child care.
