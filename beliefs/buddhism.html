EARTH RULES · BUDDHISM

Buddhism.

A fair, plain look.

Buddhists follow the teaching of the Buddha, "the Awakened One," about why we suffer and how suffering can end.

In their own words

Buddhists follow the Buddha, Siddhartha Gautama, who lived in India about 2,500 years ago. Buddha means "the Awakened One." Buddhists do not believe in a creator god. The Buddha taught the Four Noble Truths: life has suffering, craving causes it, suffering can end, and the Eightfold Path is the way. Craving causes beings to be reborn again and again. The goal is nirvana, the end of suffering. Buddhists take refuge in the Buddha, his teaching (the Dharma) and the community (the Sangha). There are three main streams. Theravada is found in South and Southeast Asia. Mahayana, which includes Zen and Pure Land, is found in East Asia. Vajrayana, or Tibetan Buddhism, uses chants called mantras and sacred drawings called mandalas.

What they believe

  • Life includes suffering, and craving is its cause.
  • Suffering can end by following the Noble Eightfold Path.
  • Beings are reborn again and again until they reach nirvana.
  • There is no creator god; every person can grow into a Buddha.
  • Mahayana Buddhists stress compassion and the bodhisattva, who helps others become free.

What they do

  • Meditation and mindfulness, alone or with a group.
  • Taking refuge in the Buddha, the Dharma and the Sangha.
  • Chanting the sutras and paying respects to the Buddha.
  • Supporting monks and nuns and learning from them.
  • Celebrating holy days like the Buddha's birthday.

How it answers the quiz

How Buddhism answers each question
QuestionAnswer
What do you think about God?There is no god. Some also say: There are many gods or divine beings.
Who was Jesus?He isn't part of what I believe. Some also say: One of many wise teachers.
What happens after we die?We are born again and again until we are freed.
What do you trust most to know what's true?A holy book plus the teaching of wise leaders over time. Some also say: What I feel inside, my conscience.
What matters most for a good life, or after it?Meditation, discipline and seeing clearly.
How much do you want a community?On holidays and big life events, and at home the rest. Some also say: I want to gather with others every week.
Who should lead a faith community?Each community chooses its own leaders. Some also say: Trained clergy, with leaders above them like bishops.
How many daily rules feel right to you?A few set practices.
How would you rather connect?Meditate, or sit in quiet thought. Some also say: Ceremonies and offerings; Pray, talking to God.
What about other beliefs?Many paths can lead to the truth. Some also say: There is one true path.
What is nature to you?Nature. Science explains it. Some also say: Sacred in itself. The seasons matter.
