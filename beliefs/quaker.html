Quakers (Religious Society of Friends).

Part of the Christianity family.

Quakers, also called Friends, believe there is that of God in everyone and seek God's guidance together, often in silent worship.

In their own words

Quakers, also called the Religious Society of Friends, believe there is "that of God in everyone," and that every person can know God directly. They call this the Inner Light. Quakers have deep Christian roots, and many try to follow the example of Jesus, though Friends hold a wide range of beliefs. In many meetings, Friends worship in silence, and anyone may stand to speak. Other Friends, including many in Kenya, have pastors, hymns and planned services. Quakers try to live out their testimonies, such as peace, truth, equality, simplicity and care for the earth.

  • How manyAbout 380,000 worldwide2017 · Friends Journal: Friends World Committee for Consultation world Quaker map (opens in a new tab)

What they believe

  • There is that of God in everyone.
  • Every person can know God directly, through the Inner Light.
  • All people are equal and of equal worth.
  • Love is at the center of life, so Friends work for peace.
  • Scripture is valued, and read alongside the Light Within.

What they do

  • Meet for worship, often in silence, where anyone may speak.
  • Make decisions together by seeking the sense of the meeting, not by voting.
  • Share leadership through volunteer clerks and committees in many meetings.
  • Live simply and work for peace, equality and care for the earth.

How it answers the quiz

How Quakers (Religious Society of Friends) answers each question
QuestionAnswer
What do you think about God?There is one God who cares about people. Some also say: The divine is in everything, or behind all things; We can't really know, or it doesn't matter.
Who was Jesus?God, or the Son of God, who saves people. Some also say: One of many wise teachers.
What happens after we die?No one can know. Some also say: We are judged, then heaven or hell.
What do you trust most to know what's true?What I feel inside, my conscience. Some also say: A holy book.
What matters most for a good life, or after it?Just being a good person. Nothing needs saving. Some also say: Faith, and God's forgiveness.
How much do you want a community?I want to gather with others every week.
Who should lead a faith community?No clergy at all. Some also say: Each community chooses its own leaders.
How many daily rules feel right to you?Very few.
How would you rather connect?Meditate, or sit in quiet thought. Some also say: Pray, talking to God.
What about other beliefs?Many paths can lead to the truth.
What is nature to you?Made by God, and ours to care for. Some also say: Sacred in itself. The seasons matter.
