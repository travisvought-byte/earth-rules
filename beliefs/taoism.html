EARTH RULES · TAOISM

Taoism.

A fair, plain look.

Taoists, also called Daoists, seek to live in harmony with the Tao, the natural Way behind all things.

In their own words

Taoism, also spelled Daoism, comes from China. The Tao, or "the Way," is the nameless source and pattern of everything. It existed before heaven and earth. Taoists try to live simply and naturally, in step with the rhythm of the world. A key idea is wu wei: acting without forcing. Yin and yang are two balancing forces, like night and day, that keep the world changing. The main book is the Tao Te Ching, linked to the sage Laozi. Taoism is both a way of thinking and a religion. Many Taoists honor gods, immortals and ancestors at temples and home altars. Many also do meditation, tai chi and qigong to balance their life energy, called qi.

What they believe

  • The Tao is the nameless source and pattern of all things.
  • Live simply and naturally, and act without forcing (wu wei).
  • Yin and yang are balancing forces that keep the world changing.
  • People and nature belong together; wisdom is following the rhythm of the world.
  • Many Taoists honor gods, immortals and their ancestors.

What they do

  • Meditation to quiet the mind.
  • Tai chi and qigong, gentle movements to balance qi, or life energy.
  • Visiting temples to burn incense and honor the deities.
  • Keeping a home altar and honoring ancestors.
  • Reading the Tao Te Ching and the Zhuangzi.

How Taoism answers each question
QuestionAnswer
What do you think about God?The divine is in everything, or behind all things. Some also say: There are many gods or divine beings.
Who was Jesus?He isn't part of what I believe.
What happens after we die?Our spirit lives on with the ancestors. Some also say: No one can know.
What do you trust most to know what's true?What I feel inside, my conscience. Some also say: A holy book plus the teaching of wise leaders over time.
What matters most for a good life, or after it?Meditation, discipline and seeing clearly. Some also say: Just being a good person. Nothing needs saving.
How much do you want a community?On holidays and big life events, and at home the rest. Some also say: Mostly on my own.
Who should lead a faith community?Each community chooses its own leaders. Some also say: No clergy at all.
How many daily rules feel right to you?Very few. Some also say: A few set practices.
How would you rather connect?Meditate, or sit in quiet thought; Ceremonies and offerings.
What about other beliefs?Many paths can lead to the truth.
What is nature to you?Sacred in itself. The seasons matter.
