Jehovah's Witnesses.

Part of the Christianity family.

Jehovah's Witnesses are Christians who worship Jehovah as the one true God and share the Bible's message about God's Kingdom.

In their own words

Jehovah's Witnesses worship one God, Jehovah, the Creator. They honor Jesus as the Son of God and their Savior, but they do not believe he is Almighty God, and they do not teach the Trinity. They believe the Bible is God's inspired message. They teach that God's Kingdom is a real government in heaven, ruled by Jesus, that will replace human governments. The dead are not in pain, and God will raise billions back to life. A group of 144,000 will rule with Jesus in heaven, and obedient people will live forever on a paradise earth. Witnesses meet twice a week and share their beliefs with others.

  • How manyAbout 9.2 million worldwide, the group says (people who share in preaching each month)2025 · Jehovah's Witnesses (jw.org): How many Jehovah's Witnesses are there worldwide? (opens in a new tab)

What they believe

  • Jehovah is the one true Almighty God and the Creator.
  • Jesus is God's Son and the Savior, but not Almighty God.
  • God's Kingdom in heaven will replace human governments.
  • The dead are not suffering; God will raise billions back to life.
  • Obedient people will live forever on a paradise earth.

What they do

  • Meet twice a week at a Kingdom Hall to study the Bible.
  • Preach the good news of God's Kingdom to others.
  • Pray, study the Bible and help people in need.
  • Avoid blood transfusions and stay neutral in politics and war.

Who was Jesus?God, or the Son of God, who saves people.
What happens after we die?We are judged, then heaven or hell.

How many daily rules feel right to you?A few set practices.
How would you rather connect?Pray, talking to God.
What about other beliefs?There is one true path.
What is nature to you?Made by God, and ours to care for.
