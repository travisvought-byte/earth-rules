EARTH RULES · HINDUISM

Hinduism.

A fair, plain look.

Hindus follow Sanatana Dharma, the "eternal way," an ancient faith from India with many paths to the divine.

In their own words

Many Hindus call their faith Sanatana Dharma, the eternal way. Hindus believe in Brahman, the one ultimate reality that is in everything. Brahman is honored in many forms, such as Vishnu, Shiva, Ganesh and the goddess Lakshmi. Each soul, called the atman, is eternal and is born again and again. Karma, the law of cause and effect, shapes each new life. The goal is moksha, freedom from the cycle of rebirth. Hindus follow different paths to that goal: devotion, knowledge, selfless work and meditation. These paths can be mixed. Hindus teach that truth is one, and the wise call it by many names.

What they believe

  • Brahman, the ultimate reality, is in everything and is honored in many forms.
  • The soul (atman) is eternal and is born again in a new body after death.
  • Karma means your actions now shape your future lives.
  • The goal is moksha, freedom from the cycle of birth and rebirth.
  • Truth is one, and other religions can also be paths to God.

What they do

  • Daily worship (puja) at a home altar with images of the deities.
  • Going to the temple on special days and for festivals.
  • Yoga and meditation, and reading holy texts like the Vedas and the Bhagavad Gita.
  • Festivals like Diwali, which marks the victory of good over evil.
  • Asking a guru, a spiritual teacher, for guidance.

How it answers the quiz

How Hinduism answers each question
QuestionAnswer
What do you think about God?The divine is in everything, or behind all things. Some also say: There are many gods or divine beings; There is one God who cares about people.
Who was Jesus?He isn't part of what I believe. Some also say: One of many wise teachers.
What happens after we die?We are born again and again until we are freed.
What do you trust most to know what's true?A holy book plus the teaching of wise leaders over time. Some also say: What I feel inside, my conscience.
What matters most for a good life, or after it?Meditation, discipline and seeing clearly. Some also say: Faith, and God's forgiveness; Following God's law and doing what is right.
How much do you want a community?On holidays and big life events, and at home the rest.
Who should lead a faith community?Each community chooses its own leaders.
How many daily rules feel right to you?A few set practices. Some also say: Many: food, clothes, prayer times.
How would you rather connect?Ceremonies and offerings. Some also say: Pray, talking to God; Meditate, or sit in quiet thought.
What about other beliefs?Many paths can lead to the truth.
What is nature to you?Sacred in itself. The seasons matter. Some also say: Made by God, and ours to care for.
