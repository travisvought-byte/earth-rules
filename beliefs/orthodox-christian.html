EARTH RULES · CHRISTIANITY

Eastern Orthodox Christianity.

Part of the Christianity family.

Orthodox Christians belong to a family of self-governing churches that keep the faith and worship handed down from the early Church.

In their own words

Orthodox Christians believe in one God who is Father, Son and Holy Spirit. They believe Jesus is God the Son, who became fully human. Through his death and rising, he frees people from sin and death. Orthodox Christians say the goal of life is theosis: growing closer and closer to God and sharing in his life, starting at baptism. They learn the faith from a living Holy Tradition that includes the Bible, the early Church councils, the saints and worship. Their churches, such as the Greek and Russian Orthodox churches, are led by bishops and patriarchs. They honor Mary as the Theotokos, the one who gave birth to God.

  • How manyNearly 260 million worldwide2010 · Pew Research Center: Orthodox Christianity in the 21st Century (opens in a new tab)

What they believe

  • One God: Father, Son and Holy Spirit.
  • Jesus is God the Son, who became fully human to save people.
  • The goal of life is theosis: growing into union with God.
  • Holy Tradition, including the Bible and the councils, guides the faith.
  • All people will rise from the dead, and Christ will judge them.

What they do

  • Gather for worship and receive Holy Communion.
  • Keep icons at home and in church as part of prayer.
  • Pray daily and fast as the Church teaches.
  • Receive the sacraments, which the Church counts as seven.

How it answers the quiz

How Eastern Orthodox Christianity answers each question
QuestionAnswer
What do you think about God?There is one God who cares about people.
Who was Jesus?God, or the Son of God, who saves people.
What happens after we die?We are judged, then heaven or hell.
What do you trust most to know what's true?A holy book plus the teaching of wise leaders over time.
What matters most for a good life, or after it?Faith, and God's forgiveness. Some also say: Meditation, discipline and seeing clearly.
How much do you want a community?I want to gather with others every week. Some also say: On holidays and big life events, and at home the rest.
Who should lead a faith community?Trained clergy, with leaders above them like bishops.
How many daily rules feel right to you?A few set practices. Some also say: Many: food, clothes, prayer times.
How would you rather connect?Pray, talking to God. Some also say: Ceremonies and offerings.
What about other beliefs?There is one true path.
What is nature to you?Made by God, and ours to care for.
