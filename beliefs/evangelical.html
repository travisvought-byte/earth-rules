EARTH RULES · CHRISTIANITY

Evangelical Protestant Christianity.

Part of the Christianity family.

Evangelicals are Protestant Christians who stress a personal faith in Jesus, the authority of the Bible, and sharing the good news.

In their own words

Evangelicals are Protestant Christians found in many different churches and groups. The word evangelical comes from a word for "good news." Evangelicals believe the Bible is God's inspired word and the highest authority for what they believe. They believe in one God: Father, Son and Holy Spirit. They believe Jesus is God, died on the cross to pay for sin, and rose from the dead. They teach that a person receives God's free gift of salvation by trusting in Jesus, often called being "born again." Sharing this good news with others is a key part of their faith.

  • How manyMore than 285 million worldwide2011 · Pew Research Center: Global Christianity, movements and denominations (citing the Center for the Study of Global Christianity) (opens in a new tab)

What they believe

  • The Bible is God's inspired word and the highest authority.
  • One God in three persons: Father, Son and Holy Spirit.
  • Jesus died on the cross for sin and rose from the dead.
  • God's free gift of salvation comes by trusting in Jesus.
  • Everyone will be raised, to life with God or to judgment.

What they do

  • Gather with a local church to worship and hear the Bible preached.
  • Read and study the Bible as the guide for faith and life.
  • Share their faith and invite others to trust in Jesus.
  • Support missions and Christian groups at school, college and work.
