EARTH RULES · BAHA'I

Baha'i Faith.

Part of the Baha'i family.

Baha'is follow Baha'u'llah and believe in one God, the oneness of religion and the oneness of all people.

In their own words

Baha'is believe in one God, who is beyond full human understanding. God has sent a series of messengers, called Manifestations of God, to teach people over time. They include the founders of the world's great religions, and most recently the Bab and Baha'u'llah, who founded the faith in Iran in the mid-1800s. Baha'is call this progressive revelation. The oneness of humankind is at the heart of the faith, so Baha'is work to end prejudice of every kind. Each person has a soul that lives on after death and keeps growing closer to God. The faith has no clergy. Elected councils guide its communities.

  • How manyMore than 5 million worldwide (the Baha'i community says)2026 · Baha'i World News Service: Statistics (opens in a new tab)

What they believe

  • There is one God, whose full nature is beyond our understanding.
  • God sends messengers over time, and Baha'u'llah is the most recent.
  • All people are one human family, and prejudice should end.
  • The soul lives on after death and keeps growing in the spiritual worlds of God.

What they do

  • Pray every day.
  • Fast from sunrise to sunset for 19 days each March.
  • Meet with the local community at the Nineteen Day Feast for prayer, news and fellowship.
  • Host or join devotional gatherings, study circles and children's classes.
  • Elect local and national councils (Spiritual Assemblies) each year.

How it answers the quiz

How Baha'i Faith answers each question
QuestionAnswer
What do you think about God?There is one God who cares about people.
Who was Jesus?A prophet or messenger of God, but not God.
What happens after we die?Our spirit lives on with the ancestors. Some also say: We are judged, then heaven or hell.
What do you trust most to know what's true?A holy book. Some also say: A holy book plus the teaching of wise leaders over time.
What matters most for a good life, or after it?Following God's law and doing what is right. Some also say: Meditation, discipline and seeing clearly.
How much do you want a community?On holidays and big life events, and at home the rest. Some also say: I want to gather with others every week.
Who should lead a faith community?No clergy at all. Some also say: Each community chooses its own leaders.
How many daily rules feel right to you?A few set practices.
How would you rather connect?Pray, talking to God. Some also say: Meditate, or sit in quiet thought.
What about other beliefs?Many paths can lead to the truth.
What is nature to you?Made by God, and ours to care for.
