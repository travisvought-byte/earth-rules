EARTH RULES · NON-RELIGIOUS

Spiritual but not religious.

A fair, plain look.

People who feel spiritual and connected to something bigger than themselves, but do not call themselves religious.

In their own words

Many people say they are spiritual but not religious. Pew Research Center found that about 1 in 5 US adults describe themselves this way. There is no founder, holy book or set of teachings. Most believe in a higher power or spiritual force, though often not God as the Bible describes. Most believe people have a soul and that something spiritual exists beyond the natural world. For many, being spiritual means being connected with their true self, with nature, and with something bigger than themselves. Some belong to a religion anyway, and slightly more do not.

  • How manyAbout 21% of US adults (Pew Research Center)2023-24 · Pew Research Center: Spiritual and religious self-descriptions, 2023-24 Religious Landscape Study (opens in a new tab)

What they believe

  • There is a higher power or spiritual force.
  • People have a soul or spirit beyond the body.
  • Many believe spirits can live in nature, like mountains, rivers and trees.
  • Being open-minded and true to yourself matters.

What they do

  • Look inward or center themselves, often a few times a month or more.
  • Some meditate to feel connected.
  • Seek a feeling of connection with nature.
  • Some pray every day.
  • Few attend religious services every week.

Find this community near you

There's no single group or place for this. Many people find others who think the same way through friends, books or local meetups.
