Secular humanism.

A fair, plain look.

Humanists live by reason, science and care for others, without belief in God or the supernatural.

In their own words

Humanism is a non-religious way of life. The American Humanist Association calls it a belief system that says we can all live good, meaningful lives without the supernatural or a god who steps in. Humanists trust science and the scientific method to learn how the world works. They make choices based on reason, empathy and concern for people and other animals. Humanists believe this life is the only one we have, so they try to find happiness in it and help others do the same. Humanists are usually atheists or agnostics, and some prefer other labels.

What they believe

  • This life is the only life we have.
  • Science and reason are the best ways to understand the world.
  • Every person has worth and basic rights.
  • People can be good without God, guided by reason and empathy.
  • People give their own lives meaning.

What they do

  • Keep thinking about their choices and growing in their values over a lifetime.
  • Try to ease suffering through kind, practical action.
  • Mark namings, weddings and funerals with ceremonies led by trained humanist celebrants.
  • Some join local humanist groups to meet others who share their views.
  • Speak up for human rights and freedom of thought.

How it answers the quiz

How Secular humanism answers each question
QuestionAnswer
What do you think about God?There is no god. Some also say: We can't really know, or it doesn't matter.
Who was Jesus?He isn't part of what I believe.
What happens after we die?Nothing. This life is all there is.
What do you trust most to know what's true?Reason, evidence and science. Some also say: What I feel inside, my conscience.
What matters most for a good life, or after it?Just being a good person. Nothing needs saving.
How much do you want a community?Mostly on my own. Some also say: On holidays and big life events, and at home the rest.
Who should lead a faith community?No clergy at all. Some also say: Each community chooses its own leaders.
How many daily rules feel right to you?Very few.
How would you rather connect?I don't need prayer or ritual.
What about other beliefs?No spiritual path is needed.
What is nature to you?Nature. Science explains it.
