EARTH RULES · PAGANISM

Modern Paganism and Wicca.

Part of the Paganism family.

Modern Pagans, including Wiccans, follow nature-centered paths inspired by the old religions of Europe and nearby lands.

In their own words

Modern Paganism is a family of new religions that grew in the late 1800s and 1900s. They draw on the old, pre-Christian religions of Europe, North Africa and West Asia. Wicca, Druidry and Heathenry are some of these paths. Pagans share a love for nature and honor the cycle of life and death. Many honor a Goddess and a God, or many gods, and some see the divine in all of nature. Most Pagans do not follow a fixed creed. Many follow a simple rule: do what you will, as long as it harms none. Pagans mark the seasons with eight yearly holidays, and many meet at the full moon. Some practice alone; others meet in small groups called covens.

  • How manyAbout 74,000 Pagans and 13,000 Wiccans in England and Wales (census)2021 · Office for National Statistics: Religion, England and Wales, Census 2021 (opens in a new tab)

What they believe

  • Nature is sacred, and people are kin to all living things.
  • The divine may be a Goddess and a God, many gods, or present in all nature.
  • Each person is responsible for their own choices: harm none.
  • Life and death move in a cycle that keeps renewing.
  • There is no single holy book or creed, so views differ.

What they do

  • Celebrating eight seasonal holidays, including the solstices.
  • Meeting for ritual at the full moon.
  • Practicing alone, or with a small group such as a coven.
  • Rituals outdoors or at a home altar, with symbols and meditation.
  • Marking life events, like handfasting, a Pagan wedding.

How it answers the quiz

How Modern Paganism and Wicca answers each question
QuestionAnswer
What do you think about God?There are many gods or divine beings. Some also say: The divine is in everything, or behind all things; There is one God who cares about people.
Who was Jesus?He isn't part of what I believe.
What happens after we die?No one can know. Some also say: We are born again and again until we are freed; Our spirit lives on with the ancestors.
What do you trust most to know what's true?What I feel inside, my conscience. Some also say: A holy book plus the teaching of wise leaders over time.
What matters most for a good life, or after it?Just being a good person. Nothing needs saving. Some also say: Meditation, discipline and seeing clearly.
How much do you want a community?On holidays and big life events, and at home the rest; Mostly on my own.
Who should lead a faith community?Each community chooses its own leaders. Some also say: No clergy at all.
How many daily rules feel right to you?Very few.
How would you rather connect?Ceremonies and offerings. Some also say: Meditate, or sit in quiet thought; Pray, talking to God.
What about other beliefs?Many paths can lead to the truth.
What is nature to you?Sacred in itself. The seasons matter.
