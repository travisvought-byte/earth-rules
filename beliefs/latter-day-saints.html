EARTH RULES · CHRISTIANITY

The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints.

Part of the Christianity family.

Latter-day Saints are Christians who believe God restored the Church of Jesus Christ through the Prophet Joseph Smith.

In their own words

Members of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints believe in God the Eternal Father, His Son Jesus Christ, and the Holy Ghost. They believe Jesus is the Savior, and that through His Atonement all people can be saved by following the gospel's laws and ordinances, such as baptism. They believe the Bible and the Book of Mormon are both the word of God, and that God still reveals His will through living prophets. All people will be resurrected and judged, and each will receive a place in one of the kingdoms of glory. Temple marriage is needed for the highest glory.

What they believe

  • God the Eternal Father, His Son Jesus Christ, and the Holy Ghost.
  • Jesus is the Savior, and His Atonement makes salvation possible for all.
  • The Bible and the Book of Mormon are both the word of God.
  • God still reveals His will through living prophets.
  • All will be resurrected and judged, then live in a kingdom of glory.

What they do

  • Keep Sunday holy for worship, rest and family.
  • Follow the Word of Wisdom: no alcohol, tobacco, tea, coffee or harmful drugs.
  • Give one-tenth of their yearly increase as tithing.
  • Serve in unpaid church callings, a few hours each week.

What do you think about God?There is one God who cares about people.
Who was Jesus?God, or the Son of God, who saves people.
What happens after we die?We are judged, then heaven or hell.

What matters most for a good life, or after it?Faith, and God's forgiveness; Following God's law and doing what is right.
How much do you want a community?I want to gather with others every week.
Who should lead a faith community?Trained clergy, with leaders above them like bishops.
How many daily rules feel right to you?Many: food, clothes, prayer times.
How would you rather connect?Pray, talking to God. Some also say: Ceremonies and offerings.
What about other beliefs?There is one true path.
What is nature to you?Made by God, and ours to care for.
