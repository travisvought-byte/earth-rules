EARTH RULES · ISLAM

Shia Islam.

Part of the Islam family.

Shia Muslims worship one God, Allah, follow the Quran and the Prophet Muhammad, and look to the Prophet's family, the Imams, for guidance.

In their own words

Shia Muslims believe in one God, Allah, and that Muhammad is God's messenger, who brought the Quran. They believe God chose Ali, the Prophet's cousin and son-in-law, and then a line of Imams from the Prophet's family to guide the faithful. Most Shia, called Twelvers, follow twelve Imams. They believe the twelfth Imam, the Mahdi, is hidden and will return to bring justice before the Last Day. While he is hidden, trusted scholars guide believers on matters of law. Shia Muslims honor the Prophet's grandson Husayn, who died at Karbala, and remember him each year on Ashura.

  • How manyBetween 162 and 211 million worldwide, about 10 to 13% of Muslims2010 · Pew Research Center: Future of the Global Muslim Population, Sunni and Shia (opens in a new tab)

What they believe

  • There is one God, Allah, who is just, and Muhammad is His messenger.
  • God chose Imams from the Prophet's family, starting with Ali, to guide people.
  • The hidden twelfth Imam, the Mahdi, will return to bring justice.
  • Jesus is a great prophet of God, not God.
  • On the Last Day the dead will rise and be judged by their deeds.

What they do

  • Pray the five daily prayers, often joined at three times of day.
  • Fast from dawn to sunset in the month of Ramadan.
  • Remember Imam Husayn on Ashura with sermons, poems and gatherings.
  • Follow the guidance of a trusted religious scholar on questions of law.
  • Make the pilgrimage to Mecca if able, and visit holy places such as Karbala.

How it answers the quiz

How Shia Islam answers each question
QuestionAnswer
What do you think about God?There is one God who cares about people.
Who was Jesus?A prophet or messenger of God, but not God.
What happens after we die?We are judged, then heaven or hell.
What do you trust most to know what's true?A holy book; A holy book plus the teaching of wise leaders over time.
What matters most for a good life, or after it?Following God's law and doing what is right. Some also say: Faith, and God's forgiveness.
How much do you want a community?I want to gather with others every week. Some also say: On holidays and big life events, and at home the rest.
Who should lead a faith community?Each community chooses its own leaders. Some also say: Trained clergy, with leaders above them like bishops.
How many daily rules feel right to you?Many: food, clothes, prayer times.
How would you rather connect?Pray, talking to God. Some also say: Ceremonies and offerings.
What about other beliefs?There is one true path.
What is nature to you?Made by God, and ours to care for.
