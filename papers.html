EARTH RULES · PAPERS

Your papers.

Replace what's lost, and keep the rest in one place.

Replace a lost ID or paper

Everywhere

Every country has an office for birth records, ID cards and passports. Start with the one where you were born for a birth record, and the one where you live for an ID.

In the United States

  • Social Security card: free, at SSA.gov (opens in a new tab).
  • Passport: State Department (opens in a new tab).
  • DD-214 for veterans: National Archives (opens in a new tab), free.

In Ohio

  • State ID card: free for Ohioans 17 and older, at any Ohio BMV (opens in a new tab) office.
  • Birth certificate: local health departments in Ohio can print one for anyone born in Ohio since 1908, usually for about $25. Call yours first.

Keep your papers in one folder

Print this list. Put each paper in one folder or envelope, and check it off. Keep it somewhere dry that you can grab fast.

  • Photo ID (driver's license or state ID)
  • Birth certificate
  • Social Security card
  • Passport, if you have one
  • Health insurance cards (Medicaid, Medicare, VA, job plan)
  • List of your medicines and doctors
  • Lease or deed
  • Car title and registration
  • Last 3 years of tax returns
  • Recent pay stubs or benefit letters
  • Bank account info (not passwords)
  • DD-214 (veterans)
  • Will, power of attorney, health care wishes
  • Copies of all of the above, kept somewhere else
