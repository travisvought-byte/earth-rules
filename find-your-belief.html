World and cultureTopic 4 of 8

How this works

  • Answer what you really think, not what you were told to think. Skip anything you're not sure about.
  • You'll see which belief systems are closest to your answers, and how close.
  • Each one is described the way its own members describe it. None is ranked above another, and not believing is a full answer too.
  • Your answers stay on your device. Nothing is saved or sent.

    A good community, of any kind

    A healthy group lets you ask questions, keep your friends and family, keep your own money, and leave when you want to. If a group, religious or not, pressures you on any of those, read Spot the trick.

    Your rights: in the United States you are free to believe anything or nothing, and to change your mind. See Religions of the world for how the law protects that.
