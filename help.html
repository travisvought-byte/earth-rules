EARTH RULES · GET HELP

Get help now.

The numbers and places to start, whatever is going on.

In danger right now

Call 911. For a mental health or suicide crisis, call or text 988. Veterans, press 1 after dialing 988.

Everything else

  • 211: food, rent, utilities, rides and local programs, by phone or at 211.org (opens in a new tab).
  • USA.gov benefits (opens in a new tab): the government's benefit finder by life event, such as a death, retirement or disability.
  • Legal Services Corporation (opens in a new tab): free civil legal aid near you.
  • Consumer Financial Protection Bureau (opens in a new tab): money problems, debt collectors and complaints.
