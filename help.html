What each one does

  • 911 sends police, fire or an ambulance. Use it when someone is in danger right now.
  • 988 is the Suicide and Crisis Lifeline. Call or text when things feel too heavy, for you or someone else. Veterans, press 1.
  • 211 connects you to local help: food, rent, utilities, rides and programs near you. You can also search at 211.org (opens in a new tab).
  • Legal Services Corporation (opens in a new tab) finds free civil legal aid near you.
  • Consumer Financial Protection Bureau (opens in a new tab) helps with debt collectors, bank problems and complaints.
  • USA.gov benefits (opens in a new tab) is the government's benefit finder by life event, such as a death, retirement or disability.
