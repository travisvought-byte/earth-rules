EARTH RULES · SCAMS

Scams.

Rush, secret, odd payment, fake official. Spot one and you've spotted them all.

The 4 signs of almost every scam

  • RushAct now or else
  • SecretDon't tell anyone
  • Odd paymentGift cards, wire, crypto, cash
  • Fake officialGovernment, bank, family

Common scams

  • Government impostor

    A call or text from the "IRS," Social Security or police says you owe money or will be arrested.

    Your move: Hang up. Agencies send letters first and never demand gift cards. Call the agency's number yourself.

  • Grandkid or family emergency

    "Grandma, I'm in jail, please don't tell Mom." Sometimes the voice is faked with AI.

    Your move: Hang up and call your grandkid or their parent at a number you know. Agree on a family code word.

  • Romance

    Someone you met online is lovely, can never video chat, and soon needs money for a ticket, a bill or an "investment."

    Your move: Never send money to someone you haven't met in person.

  • Fake job

    A job that pays well for little work, hires you without an interview, then sends a check and asks you to send some back.

    Your move: A real employer never asks you to pay or forward money.

  • Phishing

    A text or email with a link: a package problem, a locked account, a refund.

    Your move: Don't tap the link. Open the app or type the website yourself.

  • Tech support

    A pop-up or call says your computer has a virus and asks for remote access.

    Your move: Close it. Real companies don't call you about viruses.

If it happened, report it

  • Call your bank first if money left your account. The faster, the better the chance of stopping it.
  • Everyone: ReportFraud.ftc.gov (opens in a new tab).
  • Online scams: the FBI's IC3 (opens in a new tab).
  • Age 60 and older: National Elder Fraud Hotline, 833-372-8311.
  • Scam texts: forward them to 7726 (SPAM), then delete.
  • Ohio: the Ohio Attorney General, 800-282-0515.

Being scammed isn't stupid. These are run by professionals who do it all day. Telling someone is how the next person is saved.
