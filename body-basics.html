EARTH RULES · BODY BASICS

Sleep, food, boredom.

The dull basics that drive most of the big stuff.

Most bad decisions start here

Tired, hungry, lonely or bored people make worse choices about money, food, drinking, fights and phones. Recovery groups use a word for it: HALT. Hungry, angry, lonely, tired. Check those four before a big decision.

Sleep

  • Adults need 7 or more hours a night. Teens need more.
  • Same bedtime and wake time every day, weekends too.
  • Screens off and the room dark, cool and quiet.
  • Loud snoring, gasping or always tired? Ask a doctor; sleep apnea is common and treatable.

Food and moving

  • Eat mostly real food you can recognize: vegetables, fruit, beans, eggs, meat, fish and whole grains. Cut back on highly processed food and sugary drinks. Drink water.
  • Cheap and healthy: dry beans, rice, oats, eggs, frozen vegetables, bananas.
  • Move about 150 minutes a week, like a 30-minute walk five days a week. Any movement beats none.

Boredom

Boredom isn't a problem to fix with a phone. It's often the feeling right before an idea. But long stretches of nothing to do can push people toward drinking, betting and scrolling.

  • Have one thing you do with other people every week.
  • Have one thing you do with your hands: cooking, fixing, drawing, gardening.
  • When you're stuck, go outside and walk for ten minutes.
