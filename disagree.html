EARTH RULES · DISAGREE WELL

Disagree well.

Understand first. Then be understood. Winning is optional.

The goal is to understand, not to win

You'll rarely change a mind by beating it. You can understand someone, and be understood, which is what makes change possible later.

How to do it

  1. Steelman first. Say their view back in its strongest form, until they say "yes, that's it." The opposite of a straw man.
  2. Ask, don't tell. "What makes you sure?" "What would change your mind?" Questions open people up; statements shut them.
  3. Find the shared goal. Most fights are about how, not what. Safe kids, fair pay, a working town.
  4. Separate facts from values. Facts can be looked up. Values are choices. Know which one you're arguing about.
  5. Pick the moment. Not in front of a crowd, not online, not when anyone's hungry, drunk or angry.

Changing your mind without losing face

  • "I hadn't thought of that."
  • "You've changed my mind on part of this."
  • "I learned something new, so I see it differently now."
  • When someone else changes their mind, thank them. Don't say "I told you so." Make it easy, and they'll do it again.

Signs it's not a real argument

  • Name-calling, or arguing about the person instead of the point.
  • The goalposts keep moving no matter what you show.
  • Threats or yelling. You can leave. "Let's pick this up another time" is a full sentence.
