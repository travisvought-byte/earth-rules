EARTH RULES · YOUR RIGHTS

Your rights as a veteran or service member.

You served. These are yours.

Everywhere in the United States

  • You have the right to your civilian job back after service. With the seniority and pay you'd have earned, if you give notice and return on time.Source: 38 U.S. Code § 4312 (opens in a new tab)
  • You have the right to challenge a VA decision. Within one year: a Supplemental Claim with new evidence, a Higher-Level Review, or a Board Appeal.Source: 38 U.S. Code § 5104C (opens in a new tab); VA: choosing a decision review option (opens in a new tab)
  • You have the right to free help with a VA claim. Accredited veterans service officers help at no cost.Source: VA accreditation search (opens in a new tab)
  • You have the right to a copy of your own VA records. Source: Privacy Act, 5 U.S. Code § 552a(d) (opens in a new tab)
  • On active duty, you have the right to cap interest at 6% on debts from before you joined. Source: 50 U.S. Code § 3937 (opens in a new tab)
  • On active duty, you have the right to end a lease or car lease early for orders. Source: 50 U.S. Code § 3955 (opens in a new tab)
  • You have the right to veterans' preference in federal hiring. Source: 5 U.S. Code § 2108 (opens in a new tab)

In Ohio, you also have these

  • You have the right to free help from your county veterans service office. Every Ohio county has one.Source: Ohio Revised Code Chapter 5901 (opens in a new tab)
  • You have the right not to be treated differently at work or in housing because of military status. Source: Ohio Revised Code 4112.02 (opens in a new tab)

A right works best when you name it. Say it out loud, put it in writing, keep copies, and write down dates and names. Free legal help in Ohio: Ohio Legal Help (opens in a new tab). Anywhere else, dial 211 and ask for legal aid.
