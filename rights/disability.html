EARTH RULES · YOUR RIGHTS

Your rights with a disability.

Access is not a favor. It is a right.

Everywhere in the United States

  • You have the right to equal access to businesses open to the public. Stores, restaurants, hotels, doctors' offices and more must make reasonable changes so you can use them.Source: 42 U.S. Code § 12182 (opens in a new tab)
  • You have the right to equal access to government programs and services. That includes courts, schools, transit and voting.Source: 42 U.S. Code § 12132 (opens in a new tab)
  • You have the right to reasonable accommodations at work. At employers with 15 or more workers, unless it would cause undue hardship.Source: 42 U.S. Code § 12112 (opens in a new tab)
  • You have the right to bring your service dog almost anywhere the public can go. Staff can ask only two questions: is it needed because of a disability, and what task is it trained to do.Source: 28 CFR § 36.302 (opens in a new tab)
  • You have the right to fly without disability discrimination. Source: 49 U.S. Code § 41705 (opens in a new tab)
  • You have the right to reasonable accommodations and modifications in housing. Source: 42 U.S. Code § 3604(f) (opens in a new tab)

In Ohio, you also have these

  • You have the right not to be treated differently because of disability at work, in housing and in public places in Ohio. Source: Ohio Revised Code 4112.02 (opens in a new tab)
  • You have the right to be accompanied by your assistance dog in Ohio public places and transportation. Source: Ohio Revised Code 955.43 (opens in a new tab)

Complaints: ADA.gov (opens in a new tab) for access problems.

A right works best when you name it. Say it out loud, put it in writing, keep copies, and write down dates and names. Free legal help in Ohio: Ohio Legal Help (opens in a new tab). Anywhere else, dial 211 and ask for legal aid.
