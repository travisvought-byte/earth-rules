EARTH RULES · YOUR RIGHTS

Your rights as a consumer.

When you buy, borrow or owe, these rights come with you.

Everywhere in the United States

  • You have the right to see your credit reports and fix mistakes. The credit bureau generally has 30 days to investigate a dispute. Free weekly reports are at AnnualCreditReport.com (opens in a new tab).Source: 15 U.S. Code § 1681i (opens in a new tab)
  • You have the right to make a debt collector prove the debt. Dispute it in writing within 30 days of their first notice, and they must stop collecting until they send proof.Source: 15 U.S. Code § 1692g (opens in a new tab)
  • You have the right to tell a debt collector to stop contacting you. Put it in writing. They also can't harass you, call before 8 a.m. or after 9 p.m., or lie to you.Source: 15 U.S. Code § 1692c (opens in a new tab); 15 U.S. Code § 1692d (opens in a new tab)
  • You have the right to dispute a credit card billing error. Write to the card company within 60 days of the statement.Source: 15 U.S. Code § 1666 (opens in a new tab)
  • You have the right to cancel a door-to-door sale of $25 or more within three business days. Source: 16 CFR § 429.1 (opens in a new tab)
  • You have the right to stop telemarketing calls. Add your number free at DoNotCall.gov (opens in a new tab).Source: 47 U.S. Code § 227 (opens in a new tab)

In Ohio, you also have these

  • You have the right to deal with businesses that don't deceive you. Unfair or deceptive sales practices are illegal, and you can sue or complain to the Attorney General.Source: Ohio Revised Code 1345.02 (opens in a new tab)
  • You have the right to cancel a home sale until midnight of the third business day. The seller must tell you about it and refund you within 10 business days.Source: Ohio Revised Code 1345.22 (opens in a new tab); Ohio Revised Code 1345.23 (opens in a new tab)
  • You have the right to a refund or replacement for a new car that can't be fixed. That's Ohio's lemon law.Source: Ohio Revised Code 1345.72 (opens in a new tab)

Complaints: Consumer Financial Protection Bureau (opens in a new tab) for banks, credit and debt; Ohio Attorney General (opens in a new tab) for businesses in Ohio.

A right works best when you name it. Say it out loud, put it in writing, keep copies, and write down dates and names. Free legal help in Ohio: Ohio Legal Help (opens in a new tab). Anywhere else, dial 211 and ask for legal aid.
