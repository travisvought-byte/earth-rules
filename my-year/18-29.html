EARTH RULES · MY YEAR

My year: ages 18 to 29.

Print it. Check things off. Do it again next year.

Health

  • Dentist checkup and cleaning
  • Checkup with a doctor or clinic
  • Ask which vaccines you're due for
  • Eye exam if you have trouble seeing

Money

  • File taxes by April 15 (free help: VITA)
  • Check your 3 credit reports, free
  • Review insurance at open enrollment
  • Put something in emergency savings

Home and car

  • Test smoke and carbon monoxide alarms
  • Change the furnace filter
  • Renew car registration and check tires
  • Update your address everywhere if you moved

This stage of life

  • Register to vote
  • Men 18 to 25: register with Selective Service
  • Start retirement savings at your first job
  • Get your own health insurance by 26

Behind? Start here

  • No dentist in 5 years? Book one cleaning. Ohio Medicaid covers adults. Community health centers charge by income.
  • No checkup in 5 years? Book one visit. Most plans cover a yearly checkup at no extra cost.
  • Haven't filed taxes in years? You can still file. Refunds from the last 3 years can still be claimed.
  • Never looked at your credit? Pull your reports today. It doesn't hurt your score.

General guidance. Your doctor or dentist may tell you to come more or less often. Screenings by age are on Checkups.
