Behind? Start here

  • No dentist in 5 years? Book one cleaning. Ohio Medicaid covers adults. Community health centers charge by income.
  • No checkup in 5 years? Book one visit. Most plans cover a yearly checkup at no extra cost.
  • Haven't filed taxes in years? You can still file. Refunds from the last 3 years can still be claimed.
  • Never looked at your credit? Pull your reports today. It doesn't hurt your score.
